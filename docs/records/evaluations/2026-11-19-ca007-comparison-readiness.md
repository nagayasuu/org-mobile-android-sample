## 報告形式の合成再生

```text
V1 revision=r1; diff=d1; executor=verifier; command=diagnostic-check; result=failed; exit=7; errors=E_FIRST,E_LATE; warnings=W_LATE; device=unverified
V2 revision=r1; diff=d1; executor=verifier; command=report-check; result=passed; exit=0; junit_skip=1; lint_warning=1; device=unverified
```
